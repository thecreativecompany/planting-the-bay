Our Vision

A Bay Area movement, built region by region.

The strategy begins in Berkeley, then expands across San Francisco, the Peninsula, San Jose, the Tri-Valley, and beyond—through digital outreach, pop-up services, and campus ministry.

Expansion roadmap

Start focused.
Lean deeply.
Expand faithfully.

Each phase strengthens the next. The goal is not simply more locations—it is healthy leaders, strong campus ministry, and a local church community that can reproduce.

PHASE 1

Berkeley

Launch September 1 with the SF Bay Fellowship. Establish campus ministry, a local church, and the first staff team.

FIRST PLANTING
PHASE 2

San Francisco + Peninsula

Build regional gatherings, identify leaders, and create new pathways from digital outreach into local community.

EARLY EXPANSION
PHASE 3
