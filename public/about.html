Stuart & Ashley

Stuart and Ashley Mains are leading the call to plant first in Berkeley, then expand across the Bay.
See Vision
Strategy
Stuart and Ashley Mains serve as Lead Evangelist and Planting Coordinators for Planting the Bay.
Their calling is focused and practical: begin in Berkeley with the SF Bay Fellowship, build a healthy campus ministry and local church, then expand with care across the Bay.
Launching September 1. Year 1 goal: $1,000,000.
The story behind the movement

A local beginning with Baywide vision.

The vision did not begin with a desire to be everywhere at once. It began with a clear place to start: Berkeley—a city shaped by students, ideas, families, and global influence.

Stuart and Ashley are stepping into this work alongside the SF Bay Fellowship. Together, the plan is to build both campus ministry and a local church, creating a strong foundation before moving into additional Bay Area regions.

The first year is designed around presence, relationships, and repeatable ministry: meet people through digital outreach, invite them into pop-up services, and form lasting community around the Berkeley launch.

THE HEART OF THE PLAN

“Begin faithfully in Berkeley. Build a healthy local church and campus ministry. Then carry what works across the Bay.”
